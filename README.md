## What it is

A close reader with memory. It opens your manuscript, reads it the way an attentive editor would, and leaves marks for pacing, voice, rhythm, repetition, and tension. Not errors. Things worth a second look.

You teach it what you meant. The next chapter gets read with what you just taught it.

## What it isn't

Not a grammar checker. Not a prose generator. Not a chatbot. Not a first-draft assistant.

## The loop

**Notice. Teach. Continue.**

1. **It reads before it speaks.** Marks appear as you scroll. Findings, not edits.
2. **You teach it what you meant.** Dismiss a mark, explain intent, or save the pattern to your dictionary.
3. **The next pass is quieter.** What you've handled stops crowding the page. What's left is what's still worth looking at.

## What's in the kit

**Files in, files out.** Import TXT, Markdown, or DOCX. Export a revised manuscript with native Word comments your editor can answer inline. Send the file back and their replies land beside your own marks.

**A second pass (Pro).** Scene cards. Tension arc. A POV drift flag with the paragraph that broke character. An editorial letter whose claims point at the sentences they mean.

**Revision in place.** Quick-fix streams in as editable text. Apply and the page doesn't jump. Undo three deep. What you taught in Chapter 1 is still being used in Chapter 30.

## FAQ

**What stage is this for?** Finished drafts. Revision, not brainstorming.

**How does it learn?** Through the dictionary. Dismissals and intent notes save locally and carry into the next read.

**Multi-chapter novels?** Yes. The dictionary and revision memory stick with the whole manuscript.

**What if I hit a limit?** The app tells you which limit you've reached and stops that action type until it resets. Free limits are per manuscript. Pro resets monthly. Switch to BYOK to keep working while hosted limits refill.

**Cancel Pro mid-month?** Pro stays active to the end of the billing period, then drops to free. Your manuscripts, marks, dictionary, and revision history stay in your browser regardless of tier.

**Is this safe for unpublished work?** Your manuscript lives on your device. When you request an action, the relevant text goes through our proxy to the provider you picked and back. Nothing else.
